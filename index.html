Teacher Hub Teacher Hub is a content-submission app for school teachers who create learning material at home. Teachers sign in, submit lessons (videos, photos, PDFs, notes, questions, answers and compositions) and the organiser reviews each submission before it is approved. What the app does Teachers register and are approved by the organiser. Teachers submit learning content and a copyright declaration for every submission. Approved content cannot be edited or deleted by the teacher. Submitted files are stored in the organiser's Google Drive, in a folder created by the app. Teachers can add bank details for payment; changes are verified by the organiser first.

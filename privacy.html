Teacher Hub - Privacy Policy Last updated: NOVEMBER 2026 Teacher Hub ("the app") is operated by YOUR_NAME_OR_ORGANISATION ("we"). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used. Information we collect Account details: name, phone number and email address of teachers who register. Content submitted by teachers: videos, photos, PDF files, text, questions, answers and compositions, with their title, class and subject. Agreement and declaration records: the version and time at which a teacher accepted the agreement and the copyright declaration. Payment details (optional): account holder name, bank account number, IFSC code, UPI ID and phone number, provided by a teacher so that we can pay them. How we use the information To create and manage teacher accounts. To review, approve and publish learning content. To make payments to teachers after verifying their details. Google user data The app connects to the organiser's Google account only to save files that teachers submit into a folder that the app itself creates in the organiser's Google Drive (the drive.file permission). The app does not read, change or delete any other file in Google Drive. Teachers do not sign in with Google and no Google data of teachers is accessed. Teacher Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Sharing We do not sell personal information. Data is stored with our service providers (Supabase for the database and Google Drive for submitted files) only to run the app. Security and retention Access to personal and payment details is limited to the organiser. We keep data while the teacher's account is active and as needed for payment and legal records. Your choices A teacher can ask us to correct or delete their personal information by writing to us.
